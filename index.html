About me

I'm Oluwatimilehin Sapara, a Computer Science student at Texas Tech University, pursuing minors in Mathematics and Actuarial Science. I maintain a 4.0 GPA and expect to graduate in December 2027.

In Summer 2026, as an Operations Analytics Co-op at Delta Air Lines, I built a full-stack visualization tool for more than 300 KPIs and engineered an algorithm that cascades goals across hierarchies of over 20,000 nodes. This experience drives my desire to explore role which combine multiple fields within Computer Science. I also work as an Undergraduate Research Assistant at Texas Tech, applying Python, MATLAB, and machine learning to atmospheric field, wind-tunnel, and simulation data.

My interests span machine learning, data analytics, and full-stack software development. My projects include a compiler for a custom programming language, a responsive job-application platform, and a machine-learning model that predicts concrete compressive strength with 92% accuracy.

Beyond coursework and research, I serve on the ColorStack TTU Outreach Committee, where I've helped engage more than 100 students through coding workshops, mentorship, and networking initiatives. I'm also an active member of the National Society of Black Engineers and support STEM outreach for K-12 students.
